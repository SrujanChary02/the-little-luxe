Good to know

Care & questions

How do I place an order?

Tap a piece, add it to your bag, then tap Send order. Your order opens as a ready-made message — just hit send and we'll confirm availability, payment and delivery with you.

How much is shipping and how long does it take?

Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 and above; smaller orders are ₹79. We dispatch in 2–4 working days, and delivery takes 4–8 working days after that. You'll get a tracking link once it ships.

How can I pay?

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and others) or bank transfer. Cash on delivery is available on request.

Can I return or exchange a piece?

For hygiene reasons we can't take back earrings or worn pieces. If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, message us within 7 days of delivery with an unboxing photo or video and we'll replace or refund it. Full returns policy.

Will it tarnish? Can I wear it in water?

Our pieces have an anti-tarnish finish made for daily wear, but no plated jewelry lasts forever if it's soaked often. Take it off for swimming, the gym and long showers, and it will keep its shine much longer.

Jewelry care guide
  • Last on, first off. Put jewelry on after perfume, lotion and hairspray.
  • Keep it dry. Remove before swimming, bathing or workouts.
  • Wipe it gently with a soft dry cloth after wearing.
  • Store it separately in the pouch or a zip bag, away from humidity.
  • Avoid chemicals like sanitiser and cleaning sprays on your pieces.
Do you do gift wrapping?

Yes! Tick Gift wrap in your bag (₹49) and add a note. We'll leave the price out of the parcel.
