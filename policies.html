Shipping & delivery

  • We ship across India.
  • Orders of ₹999 and above ship free. Smaller orders have a flat shipping charge of ₹79.
  • Orders are dispatched within 2–4 working days of payment confirmation, and usually arrive 4–8 working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • You'll receive a tracking link once your order ships.
  • Delays caused by the courier, weather or public holidays are outside our control, but we'll always help you follow up.

Returns, exchanges & refunds

  • For hygiene reasons, earrings and pieces that have been worn can't be returned or exchanged.
  • If your order arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, message us within 7 days of delivery with an unboxing video or clear photos of the item and packaging.
  • Once we've confirmed the issue, we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund (including shipping) — your choice, subject to stock.
  • Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
  • Orders can be cancelled before they're dispatched for a full refund. Once shipped, an order can't be cancelled.

Privacy

  • When you order, we use your name, phone number and delivery address only to process, ship and support your order.
  • We never sell or share your details with anyone except the courier delivering your parcel.
  • This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your shopping bag is saved only in your own browser.
  • Ask us at any time to see or delete the details we hold about you.

Terms

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹). Orders are confirmed once payment is received.
  • Product photos are as accurate as we can make them; small variations in colour and finish are natural.
  • Our jewelry is fashion jewelry with an anti-tarnish finish — not solid gold or silver. With the care tips on our site it will stay beautiful for a long time.
  • We may update prices, products and these policies from time to time. The version on this page when you order applies to your order.

Contact

For any question about an order, message us and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
